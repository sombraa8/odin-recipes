Cafe Latte


Learn how to make a cafe latte with this easy recipe that will please all fellow coffee drinkers!

Taking only 15 minutes, this recipe is perfect for those who enjoy the experience provided by cafe lattes without the absurd price!

Ingredients

  • 2 cups milk
  • 1⅓ cups hot freshly brewed dark roast espresso coffee

Steps

  1. Gather the ingredients.
  2. Heat milk in a saucepan set over medium-low heat. Whisk briskly with a wire whisk to create foam.
  3. Brew espresso and pour into four cups. Pour in milk, holding back the foam with a spoon. Spoon foam over the top.
  4. Enjoy.

Nutrition Facts (per serving)

  • 4 Servings
  • 63 Calories
  • 3g Fat
  • 6g Carbs
  • 4g Protein
