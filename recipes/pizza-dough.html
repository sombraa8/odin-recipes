Pizza Dough

Delicious, quick, and easy homemade pizza dough recipe.

This simple recipe is perfect for an amazing and quick dinner on a budget, having very few ingredients and taking only 1 hour and 10 minutes total! The pizza dough is compatible with any toppings of your choice, and is able to serve many people.

This recipe is great when craving pizza while not desiring to spend multiple hours working on it!

Ingredients

  • ¼ ounce active dry yeast
  • 2 teaspoons granulated sugar
  • ¾ cup warm water (110°F/45°C)
  • 2 cups bread flour, plus more as needed
  • 1 teaspoon kosher salt
  • 3 tablespoon extra-virgin olive oil, divided

Steps

  1. Gather all ingredients.
  2. Stir yeast, sugar, and warm water together in a small bowl. Let stand until foamy, about 5 minutes.
  3. Stir 2 cups of bread flour and salt together in the bowl of a stand mixer. Add yeast mixture and 2 tablespoons of olive oil; beat with dough hook on medium speed until dough is soft and slightly tacky, about 8 minutes, adding up to 3 tablespoons more flour, 1 tablespoon at a time, mixing after each addition.
  4. Cover and let rise at room temperature until doubled in volume, at least 1 hour or up to 2 hours. (Alternatively, cover and let rise in the refrigerator overnight.)
  5. Preheat the oven to 475°F (245°C) with oven rack positioned in lower third. Turn dough out onto a lightly floured work surface. Shape into a round and roll into a thin crust. Place on a large parchment-lined baking sheet or pizza pan.
  6. Top with desired pizza sauce and toppings. Brush edges with remaining 1 tablespoon oil.
  7. Bake in preheated oven until crust is golden and cheese is melted bubbly, about 12 to 15 minutes.
  8. Serve and enjoy.
